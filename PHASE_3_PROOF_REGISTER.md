# Alvora Phase 3 Proof Register

## Purpose

This register is the publication gate for public trust, capability, certification, fulfilment, and commercial claims. A claim may appear on the public site only when the evidence source is available, the wording is approved, and the responsible owner has confirmed that the statement remains current.

The register deliberately distinguishes **publicly observable product/process information** from **business credentials that require internal evidence**. A page may describe the route and the buyer’s next verification step without presenting an unverified credential as proof.

## Claim controls

| Claim area | Public status | Permitted public wording | Evidence required before stronger wording | Owner / review cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Surat manufacturing origin | Publish with operational confirmation | “Made in Surat” or “Surat manufacturing” when the current production operation confirms it | Current legal/operating address or facility confirmation | Operations / quarterly |
| Certified or report-linked stones | Publish only per listed item/order | “Certification/report destination shown where supplied” and “confirm the applicable report before dispatch” | Current report number, trusted certificate URL, and item-level mapping | Catalogue owner / every import |
| IGI laser inscription/database validation | Do not generalize to every stone without evidence | “IGI report destination shown where supplied” | Item-level report evidence and documented validation process | Quality owner / every import and quarterly |
| Excellent/Ideal, no fluorescence, no BGM standard | Publish as a stated standard, not universal fact | “Alvora’s stated standard make is Excellent/Ideal cut, no fluorescence, no BGM” | Current production specification and quality-control record | Production owner / quarterly |
| Calibration and matched parcels | Publish as a capability when the team confirms scope | “Calibrated profiles” and “matched layouts/parcels” | Current accepted examples, specification template, or QC record | Production owner / quarterly |
| Custom make timing | Publish only as a qualified estimate | “Typical specification-make timing is 5–10 working days; confirm current capacity” | Current capacity and lead-time record by project type | Operations / weekly during active campaigns |
| Recut, repolish, rework, or replacement | Publish as a scoped handling route | “Qualifying Alvora-made stones can be assessed; confirm the correction path and written order terms” | Written policy and order terms defining eligibility and process | Operations/legal review / quarterly |
| Payment terms | Publish as a conversation, not a promise | “Negotiated trade terms may be available after credit and reference checks” | Current credit policy and approval owner | Finance/operations / quarterly |
| Export and shipment documentation | Publish only by destination and order | “Shipment and documentation details are confirmed against the destination and written order” | Destination-specific logistics/customs review and current partner process | Export owner / per destination/order |
| 25+ years’ experience | Hold pending evidence | Do not publish the number until verified | Company history record or approved founder/business documentation | Business owner / annual |
| 10,000+ stones dispatched | Hold pending evidence | Do not publish the number until verified | Dispatch ledger or auditable operational summary | Operations / quarterly |
| 100% IGI standard on every stone | Remove from public proof | Do not use as a universal claim | Stone-level evidence and approved legal wording; otherwise permanently avoid | Quality/legal / quarterly |
| Direct-from-bench pricing | Qualify before publication | Use “direct production conversation” rather than a pricing guarantee | Current pricing model and approved commercial explanation | Commercial owner / quarterly |
| Named offices, global presence, partners, testimonials, or customer results | Hold until permission/evidence | Publish only the specific verified relationship or approved anonymized result | Permission, source record, and expiry/review date | Marketing/business owner / before publication |

## Publication checklist

Before releasing or refreshing a page, the owner must confirm the applicable claim rows, attach or link the evidence source, record the date checked, and use the permitted wording when evidence is incomplete. Certification, report, timing, MOQ, export, payment, and correction language must be checked against the current order process rather than copied from an older page.

Customer names, photographs, production volumes, savings, reorder results, CAD, and confidential design information require explicit permission or an approved anonymized version. Analytics events must contain only non-personal attribution and page context; never place a buyer’s name, email, company, budget, CAD, or design details in event properties.

## Current implementation note

The public homepage now presents a buyer-verifiable proof frame—specification, supplied report destination, technical review, and written order/dispatch terms—in place of the unverified numerical proof strip. Correction language is scoped to qualifying Alvora-made stones and directs the buyer to confirm the written order terms. The existing B2B and Insights pages use the same evidence-first posture.
