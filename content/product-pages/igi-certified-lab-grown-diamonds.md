IGI identifies a stone as laboratory-grown and offers report verification and laser inscription linking the report number to the stone.[1] Its familiar 4Cs-oriented format is practical for comparing trade inventory. The report is an independent grading opinion, not a warranty, appraisal, or substitute for shipment-level QC.

Growth method and treatment can matter to a professional buyer. CVD and HPHT are different production routes, and a finished stone may require treatment or additional disclosure. IGI states that growth method and treatments may be recorded in report comments when requested.[1] Buyers should specify the disclosure they need instead of inferring method or treatment from appearance, colour, or price.

For layouts and pairs, the report evaluates each individual diamond; it does not automatically confirm that the group fits a design. Alvora's specification-led brief can therefore include measurements, matching criteria, production reference, approval images, and report linkage. GIA may be appropriate when a channel requires its name, while GCAL can be considered for performance-led stones where optical evidence is central.[2]
