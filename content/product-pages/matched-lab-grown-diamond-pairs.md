Alvora's matched lab-grown diamond pairs are two stones selected to work together by measurement, outline, colour, clarity, cut appearance, and overall visual balance. They are intended for earrings, paired accents, side-by-side settings, and repeat designs where an uneven pair can affect the finished jewellery.

## Key facts

- Certification: IGI certification can provide report-linked identity and familiar 4Cs information where applicable.
- Tolerance range: Matched within 1 colour grade, 1 clarity grade, and 2% weight variance between the two stones; define the permitted difference between the two stones for each required measurement. <!-- DRAFT-VALUE: confirm with Akshay -->
- MOQ: 1 pair (2 stones) minimum; clarify whether one pair, a sample pair, or a repeat programme is the minimum. <!-- DRAFT-VALUE: confirm with Akshay -->
- Lead time: 7–10 working days; confirm availability, matching, certification, approval, and dispatch timing. <!-- DRAFT-VALUE: confirm with Akshay -->
- Primary shapes: rounds, ovals, pears, marquises, emeralds, radiants, cushions, hearts, and agreed fancy shapes.

Pair matching is not simply a request for equal carat weights. Two stones can weigh the same while differing in length, width, depth, faceting, fluorescence, or face-up appearance. Those differences can affect symmetry, orientation, light return, and how each stone sits in its mounting.

A useful brief states the finished-design requirement. Share the shape, nominal dimensions, colour and clarity range, setting style, quantity, and any CAD or reference stone. The matching rule may include measurements, outline, table and depth relationships, polish and symmetry, visual tone, and the acceptable range between the stones. Explicit criteria are more useful than an undefined promise of "matching."

IGI reports can identify individual laboratory-grown stones and support report verification.[1] The report remains one documentation layer: it does not certify that two stones form a visually and dimensionally coherent pair. Approval images or video showing the pair together can help the buyer, setter, and designer review the result before shipment.
