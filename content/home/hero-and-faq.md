## FAQ

### 1. Do you supply lab-grown diamonds directly from Surat?

Yes. Alvora is a manufacturing operation in Surat, Gujarat, India, founded in 2000. The business supplies certified lab-grown diamonds for jewellery businesses and can discuss standard stones, calibrated layouts, matched pairs, melee parcels, and custom-cut requirements against a written specification.

### 2. Can you make calibrated diamond layouts to my measurements?

Yes, subject to feasibility review. Send the shape, nominal measurements, quantity, colour and clarity range, setting architecture, and any CAD or reference file. For the current layout brief, the draft acceptance value is ±0.05 mm on diameter or length-width; this value remains marked for confirmation and should be agreed before production.

### 3. Can you supply matched lab-grown diamond pairs?

Yes. A matched-pair brief can cover shape, dimensions, outline, colour, clarity, cut appearance, setting style, quantity, and visual balance. The current draft matching value is within 1 colour grade, 1 clarity grade, and 2% weight variance between the two stones; this remains subject to confirmation in the order brief.

### 4. Do you make custom-cut lab-grown diamonds from a CAD file?

Alvora can review custom-cut requirements from a CAD file, drawing, reference stone, or written brief. The review should identify finished dimensions, outline, facet direction, setting constraints, quantity, colour, clarity, and which requirements must remain consistent across repeat production. Feasibility depends on the agreed design and available production route.

### 5. Are your diamonds IGI or GIA certified?

Alvora's confirmed certification bodies are IGI and GIA. The required report route should be stated when requesting a quote, because certification needs can differ by stone type, destination market, retailer, and product brief. Report-linked identity and the finished-stone specification should be reviewed together.

### 6. Do you work with both CVD and HPHT diamonds?

Yes. Alvora works across CVD and HPHT. The method should be selected and disclosed according to the finished-stone requirement, treatment disclosure, size, shape, colour, clarity, and supply brief. CVD or HPHT should not be treated as a universal quality ranking on its own.

### 7. What is the minimum order quantity?

The draft minimums are product-specific: no minimum for a single certified stone available ex-stock; 5 carats total weight for a calibrated layout; 1 pair, or 2 stones, for matched pairs; 5 carats total for a custom-cut production run; and 10 carats for a melee parcel. These figures are draft values and must be confirmed with the quotation.

### 8. How quickly can you dispatch?

For stock, standard, and precision-wholesale requirements, the confirmed planning statement is 2–3 working days to dispatch. Bespoke calibrated layouts and custom cuts have a draft planning window of 7–10 working days for cutting and certification, followed by buyer approval and dispatch. The applicable timing should be confirmed against the exact brief.

### 9. Do you offer a rework guarantee if the stones do not meet the brief?

Do not assume a blanket rework guarantee. The Master Input Sheet does not confirm a universal rework policy, so any correction, rework, replacement, approval condition, or acceptance remedy should be written into the quotation or production confirmation for the specific order. This keeps the commercial and technical obligation clear for both sides.

### 10. Which jewellery businesses are you set up to supply?

The homepage is intended for jewellery manufacturers, wholesalers, ateliers, and private-label businesses. It is most useful when the buyer has a defined requirement for certified stones, calibrated layouts, matched pairs, melee, or a custom cut. Include the intended quantity, design reference, certification requirement, destination market, and timing so the enquiry can be assessed as a production brief.
