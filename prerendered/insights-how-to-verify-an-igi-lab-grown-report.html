How to Verify an IGI Lab-Grown Diamond Report Before You Buy

25 September 2026 · Alvora Diamonds editorial team · 3 min read · Last reviewed 26 September 2026

The short answer

An IGI lab-grown diamond report is verified by entering the report number on IGI's verification page, matching the laser inscription on the stone's girdle to that report, and confirming that weight, measurements and grades agree exactly.

What this means for a buyer: Verify every report number on IGI's own site and match the laser inscription before you pay; it takes minutes per stone.

Key takeaways

  • Verify the report number on IGI's official verification page, not a screenshot or a PDF forwarded alone.
  • The report number is laser-inscribed on the girdle; inscription and report should always travel together.
  • Weight, measurements and grades on the report should match the stone exactly.
  • A certificate describes one stone; parcel invoices should reconcile every stone to its own report number.

Step 1: check the report online

Evidence: Verified public evidence

Every IGI report carries a unique number. Enter it on IGI's verification page and read the record directly from the laboratory: carat weight, colour, clarity, cut or polish and symmetry grades, measurements, fluorescence, growth method and the laboratory-grown origin statement.

Treat the online record as the source of truth. A printed or forwarded report is a copy; the database entry is the report.

Step 2: match the laser inscription

Evidence: Verified public evidence

IGI laser-inscribes the report number on the stone's girdle. Under a jeweller's loupe, the inscription on the stone should read exactly the number you verified online. An uninscribed stone, or an inscription that does not match, stops the purchase until explained.

Step 3: reconcile stone and paperwork

Evidence: Alvora process

The verified record should describe the stone in hand: same weight to the hundredth, same measurements, same grades. For parcels and layouts, the invoice should list each stone against its own report number so every stone can be checked individually at goods-in.

Alvora stones are IGI laser-inscribed and validated against the IGI database before dispatch, and every stone ships with its own IGI or GIA report. Where a certificate link is shown on the site, it points to a matching official laboratory destination.
